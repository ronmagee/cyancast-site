The problem

The floor decides what works

Restaurant sound is a balancing act: loud enough to set the mood, clear enough for calls that matter.

✕Ambience that clashes

Bar and dining room forced into one playlist, one volume.

✕Kitchen calls get lost

Ticket times and name-calls buried under the music.

✕No flexibility at service

Changing the mood for a private event means reconfiguring the wiring.
