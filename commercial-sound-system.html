Scalable by design

Traditional Bluetooth caps at a handful of speakers. CyanCast's broadcast protocol removes that ceiling — add terminals as your space grows.

  • ∞
    Unlimited wireless terminalsBluetooth broadcast protocol breaks the old 1-to-1 Bluetooth limit — connect as many receivers as you need.
  • 120
    Up to 120 m coverageHigh-gain antenna and signal boost cover open spaces like stores, campuses and factories.
  • ∇
    Low-latency syncOptimized codec keeps every speaker in perfect sync — ideal for live voice paging.
  • ★
    MIC priority (auto-mute)When you speak into the gooseneck mic, background music duck automatically, then resumes.
  • A/B
    A/B independent zonesControl two areas separately — play music at the front desk, mute the warehouse.
  • 🔒
    Secure private networkReceivers stay undiscoverable by default; pair manually to keep outsiders off your system.
