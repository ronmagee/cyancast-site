About CyanCast

We build wireless commercial audio that simply works — for the spaces people actually use every day.

Get a Quote View the Hub

Our focus

CyanCast is a commercial audio brand with one job done well: wireless commercial sound. Our flagship Desktop Wireless Audio Hub replaces runs of speaker cable and racks of amplifiers with a single host that broadcasts to an unlimited number of terminals — up to 120 m away.

We design for business environments where voice must be heard clearly and music must stay in its place: houses of worship, schools, hotels, retail, factories and corporate spaces.

Two entities, one product

CyanCast and its manufacturing arm are two separate companies — and we keep them that way on purpose.

Brand & Trade

CyanCast

The brand and trading company. Positioned as a Singapore-based trading company, CyanCast owns the brand and leads international sales, distribution and partner relationships — built to serve overseas markets cleanly and professionally. CyanCast is Singapore-based and its trade promotion is led by Ron Magee, who handles partner and buyer contact.

R&D & Manufacturing

Xi Yan Broadcasting

Based in Guangzhou, Xi Yan Broadcasting handles research, development and manufacturing — the wireless protocol, the hardware, the build quality. The making is theirs; the brand is CyanCast's.

This separation means partners buy a brand they can stand behind, backed by a dedicated engineering and manufacturing team — not a faceless white-label.

What sets us apart
