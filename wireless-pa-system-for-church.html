The problem

Church audio should not need a construction project

Every worship space ends up with the same three headaches. None of them are about the music.

✕Runs of cable

Threading speaker wire through historic walls, plaster and pillars takes weeks and permanent damage.

✕Back pews that hear less

Speech fades before it reaches the rear rows — the furthest seats get the least of the sermon.

✕One switch for everything

Sanctuary, hall and courtyard all share a single volume. There is no way to speak to just one area.

The system

One host. Every pew, hall and courtyard.

The CyanCast Hub sits at the pulpit and broadcasts to wireless terminals around the building. No cable run, no rewiring, no contractor.
